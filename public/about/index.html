Bounce is a song sketchpad in your browser. Write a progression, put drums under it, track a scratch vocal and get the lyrics down. When the idea’s there, export stems and MIDI and finish it in your DAW.

Track it

Scratch vocals, as many takes as you need

Hit record and sing over the loop, with a count-in to get you in. Every take is kept, so you can pick the best one, trim its ends on the timeline, and stack harmonies on tracks of their own. A quick timing check keeps your takes in time with the track.

Write it down

Lyrics, synced to the sections

Each section gets its own lyrics, with its chords above them and a syllable count on every line to help with phrasing. The lyric sheet follows the playhead, so you always see the section you’re in.

Shape it

Arrange the whole song

Lay out verses, choruses and a bridge, reorder them by dragging, and reuse a section wherever it repeats. Modulate any section, like a key change into the last chorus, and see the form and length at a glance.

Chords that fit your key

Build progressions from the diatonic chords, with 7ths, sus and borrowed chords a click away. Pick a sound, voicing and arpeggiator pattern.

Grooves to write over

Drum patterns from four-on-the-floor to half-time to drum & bass, with fills and a sparse, normal or busy feel for each section.

Start from a known progression

Open a template built on a progression everyone knows, from “Creep” to “I Feel Love”, and change it until it’s yours. Or start blank.

On your phone, too

The full app works on a phone, with a chord pad and one-tap recording. Catch the idea when it turns up, then pick it up at your desk.

Export to your DAW

Bounce a WAV mix to share, or export stems and MIDI for Logic, Ableton, FL Studio or any DAW. Tempo and key come along, so everything lines up.

Free, with no account

Nothing to sign up for or install. Sessions save in your browser, and you can back one up, recordings included, to a single file.
