# Roadmap

Notes for future updates: things to fix or add, gathered while using Bounce.

## To do

- **Delete or duplicate a section from inside it.** It should be easy to
  delete or duplicate the open section from the section itself, without
  going back to the song view or the tab's menu.
- **Make an active mute easier to see.** A track's mute button is hard to
  see when it's on.
- **Mute vocals straight away.** Muting a vocal track should silence it the
  moment it's pressed. Right now a vocal keeps playing until the section
  finishes.
